## How Users Get Access

The Only Path:

1. Creator creates circle
1. Creator receives unique invite link from success modal
1. Creator manually shares link via:

- Private message
- Email
- WhatsApp/Telegram
- Social media DM
- Any private channel

1. Only recipients of that link can access the Join page
1. Once on Join page, they see preview and can join if circle not full

## Security Through Obscurity

What Protects Your Circle:

1. Unguessable URLs: CircleId is likely a large number or hash
   
  
   https://chaincircle.app/join/0x7f9c...8a3b (or similar)
   1. No Public Directory: No way to enumerate or list circles
1. Selective Sharing: You control who receives the link
1. First-Come, First-Served with Limit: maxMembers cap (3-12) prevents unlimited joins
